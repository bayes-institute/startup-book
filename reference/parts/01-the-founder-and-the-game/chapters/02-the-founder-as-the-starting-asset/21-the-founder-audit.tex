\section{The Founder Audit: A Method to Complete}

\subsection{5 Progressive Passes}

A first-time founder should be able to complete this audit in 30 days, producing empirical receipts rather than a motivational autobiography. The audit proceeds through 5 progressive passes:

\subsection{Pass 1: Independent Asset Inventory}

Set aside 4 hours without a pitch deck, cofounder, or mentor. Work from hard receipts (old code repositories, work artifacts, calendars, invoices, published outputs, feedback, and personal financial statements).

\subsection{Pass 2: External Triangulation}

Interview 6 to 10 people who have closely observed your work: managers, direct reports, peers, students, or clients. Include at least 2 who are skeptical and unlikely to flatter you.

\textbf{Do not ask friends ``Do you think I should be a founder?'' Ask: ``Which specific responsibilities would you hesitate to give me without supervision?''}

Request specific episodes, not adjectives:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=11pt,itemsep=1pt,topsep=1pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item Which difficult task have you seen me perform unusually well?
  \item When have you observed me struggle or fail? What caused it?
  \item If you had to hire me for 1 paid freelance project, what would it be?
  \item Have you seen me make a costly mistake? How did I handle it?
  \item What problem do you think I understand better than my peers?
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Pass 3: 3 Observable Demonstrations}

Select 1 tangible work sample for each of 3 functions:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.},leftmargin=11pt,itemsep=1.5pt,topsep=1.5pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{Create:} Build a working tool or diagnostic without employer infrastructure.
  \item \textbf{Persuade:} Explain the value proposition clearly to an unconvinced practitioner.
  \item \textbf{Operate:} Deliver a bounded result under tight time and quality bounds.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Pass 4: Confront Constraints}

Audit available cash, 6-month reserves, weekly hours, employer non-competes, and intellectual property terms.

\textbf{Confronting your constraints and deciding not to quit your job is not a failure; it is a rational strategic decision that preserves your runway to experiment safely.}

\subsection{Pass 5: Map 3 Opportunities and Test 1}

Synthesize your assets into 3 opportunity cards. Reject any idea that breaches a hard gate. Choose the single candidate whose falsification experiment is most accessible within 14 days.
