\section{A 2-Week Founding Architecture Process}

\subsection{Decision Velocity Over Procrastination}

A founder does not require 6 months of solitary philosophical retreat to articulate an institutional constitution. In fact, excessive linguistic polishing prior to real-world customer contact is simply an elegant form of entrepreneurial procrastination.

The objective of a rapid founding architecture process is \textbf{operational decision clarity}, not eternal metaphysical certainty.

\subsection{The Post-Sprint Diagnostic State}

Upon completing this 2-week architecture sprint, the founder must be equipped to answer 8 questions with unhesitating clarity:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=12pt,itemsep=2pt,topsep=1pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item Why do I desire to build an enterprise, and what constitutes personal victory?
  \item What enduring positive contribution does the institution exist to advance?
  \item What kind of institution might it become over a 15-year horizon?
  \item Which commercial shortcuts and marketing tactics are categorically forbidden?
  \item Which operational constraints are immutable, and which are temporary stage limitations?
  \item Which business models are acceptable for immediate testing?
  \item Why is our initial 90-day experiment a coherent stepping stone toward the mission?
  \item What empirical market facts do we currently lack, and how will Part~II discover them?
\end{enumerate}

The objective is not that every answer remains carved in granite for decades. The objective is that the founder never again confuses an ethical conviction, a strategic choice, and an unverified market hypothesis.
