\section{The Method-Selection Matrix: Context Dictates the Tool}

\subsection{The Hazard of Methodological Dogmatism}

Venture advice is often paralyzed by universal prescriptions. Lean startup advocates demand continuous rapid A/B experimentation for every problem. Corporate strategists insist on multi-year discounted cash flow pro-formas. Traditional engineers demand exhaustive waterfall specifications.

Every one of these tools is powerful within its native operating environment, and actively destructive when applied outside it. The calibrated founder matches the analytical instrument to the feedback speed, sample density, and irreversibility of the decision domain.

Consider 5 radically different operational problems:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=12pt,itemsep=2pt,topsep=1pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{Consumer Goods Packaging:} High transaction volume, rapid retail feedback, low unit cost. Direct consumer testing and multivariate shelf experiments yield actionable statistical significance within weeks.
  \item \textbf{Early-Stage Enterprise Software:} An early B2B product with 30 total users. Running statistical A/B tests on button colors is statistically meaningless. In-depth user observation sessions and direct diagnostic interviews are infinitely superior.
  \item \textbf{High-End Specialized Consulting:} Complex multi-stakeholder procurement with ₹50-lakh contract values. Evidence is generated through paid diagnostic discovery scopes and executive workshops, not algorithmic marketing funnels.
  \item \textbf{Industrial Advanced Manufacturing:} Constructing a specialized semiconductor fabrication facility requiring ₹500 crore of irreversible physical capital. \textit{``Move fast and break things''} is corporate suicide. The environment demands rigorous finite-element modeling, stress-tested supply agreements, and staged capital milestones.
  \item \textbf{Foundational Scientific Laboratory:} Investigating novel quantum computing architectures. Scientific uncertainty is radical and irreducible. Success depends on portfolio bets, independent peer review, and milestone-governed patient capital.
\end{enumerate}
