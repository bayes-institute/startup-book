\subsection*{Appendix I: The First 60 Days of Field Discovery}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.3ex]
  \item \textbf{Days 1 to 7: Formulate Falsifiable Hypotheses.} Define 3 candidate customer situations. For each, name the trigger, job, current alternative, budget owner, and 1 observation that would prove the market does not exist. Do not build landing pages.
  \item \textbf{Days 8 to 18: Reconstruct Past Behavior.} Conduct 12 to 15 forensic historical interviews with recent buyers and nonbuyers. Document actual workarounds and recorded financial expenditures.
  \item \textbf{Days 19 to 28: Challenge Emerging Theories.} Intentionally seek out negative, disconfirming cases: people who solved the issue with free tools and managers who rejected external vendors. Update the Contrast Matrix.
  \item \textbf{Days 29 to 40: Execute an Atomic Commercial Test.} Offer a bounded, precisely scoped paid pilot (e.g., a 2-day evaluation clinic or diagnostic audit) to qualified prospects at full market price. Predefine numerical disproof thresholds.
  \item \textbf{Days 41 to 50: Audit Live Delivery Friction.} Measure actual customer activation, student cognitive struggle, instructor grading burden, and post-purchase feedback. Track dropouts and refund requests unsparingly.
  \item \textbf{Days 51 to 60: Synthesize and Select the Beachhead.} Formulate the definitive Causal Customer Statement. Select the 1 narrow customer segment for initial scaling in Chapter 7, and archive rejected ideas.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Appendix J: The Weekly Customer-Learning Protocol}

Even a solo founder should reserve 30 minutes every Friday to answer these 7 governance questions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.2ex]
  \item What did our prospective customers \textbf{actually do} this week (not what did they say)?
  \item What fact completely surprised us or contradicted our primary thesis?
  \item What is the strongest reason our customers continue to use their current workaround?
  \item Which stakeholder role or veto holder remains unverified in our pipeline?
  \item What did we learn from the last prospect who explicitly said ``no''?
  \item How was our research contaminated by our own enthusiasm or leading questions?
  \item What concrete operational change will we make to next week's product offering?
\end{enumerate}
