\subsection*{Appendix B: The 45-Minute Buyer-History Protocol}

\noindent\textbf{Preparation:} Confirm the subject has recently experienced or decided upon the specific problem. Disclose recording, ensure privacy, and emphasize: ``We are here to discover how decisions actually happen, not to pitch a product.''

\noindent\textbf{1. Context and Incident (10 mins):}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.2ex]
  \item ``When did this problem first become serious enough that you felt forced to take action?''
  \item ``What was the calendar deadline, and what were the consequences of missing it?''
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{2. The Existing Workaround (10 mins):}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.2ex]
  \item ``What was the first thing you tried? Show me the script or notes if possible.''
  \item ``How many hours did you spend on this last week? Who else was dragged into it?''
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{3. The Search and Alternative Journey (10 mins):}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.2ex]
  \item ``What commercial options did you look at? How did you find them?''
  \item ``Which 2 made your shortlist, and why was 1 eliminated?''
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{4. Procurement and Commitments (10 mins):}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.2ex]
  \item ``Who approved the expense? What budget line did it come from?''
  \item ``What legal, security, or procurement hurdles almost killed the deal?''
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{5. Outcome and Synthesis (5 mins):}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.2ex]
  \item ``What remains broken today? Knowing what you know now, what would you do differently?''
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Appendix C: 3 Specialized Interview Paths for Bayes Institute}

\subsubsection*{C1. Working Software Developer / Technical Analyst}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.2ex]
  \item Walk me through the last AI feature you shipped. What failed in production testing?
  \item When evaluation metrics looked flawed, who did you consult first?
  \item What external resources did you evaluate: books, open repos, YouTube, bootcamps?
  \item How many hours did you realistically have available outside your core 40-hour work week?
  \item If an unknown institute offered code reviews on your evaluation pipeline, what evidence would prove the instructor is world-class?
  \item What would prevent you from finishing an intensive 6-week studio: family, work, or prerequisites?
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{C2. College Academic Leader / Department Head}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.2ex]
  \item What was the last externally delivered technical module your department integrated?
  \item Who initiated the proposal, and which academic board approved it?
  \item How were faculty members involved, and what workload concerns did they raise?
  \item What were students' actual programming prerequisites, and how were gaps handled?
  \item What was the financial contract structure: per student, per section, or fixed fee?
  \item When does the next academic council convene to approve upcoming semester electives?
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{C3. Enterprise Engineering Director / VP}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.2ex]
  \item Which AI-enabled workflow in your engineering organization is currently underperforming?
  \item What proportion of the bottleneck is software tooling vs. developer systems judgment?
  \item What have internal senior architects already tried to fix the issue?
  \item What time can your developers realistically dedicate to an applied evaluation clinic during work hours?
  \item Who signs off on vendor security and confidentiality before external trainers can view architecture diagrams?
  \item What measurable milestone in sprint velocity or defect reduction would justify renewing a pilot?
\end{enumerate}
