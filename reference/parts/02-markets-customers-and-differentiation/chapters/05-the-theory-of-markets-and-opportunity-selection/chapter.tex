\ChapterOpener{5}{The Theory of Markets and Opportunity Selection}{How to Discover Where Valuable Demand Exists, Where Profits Can Survive, and Which Opportunity to Pursue}{A good market is not simply one with many potential customers, fast growth, or weak competition. It is a setting in which a particular group has a consequential job to accomplish, can and will commit resources to accomplishing it, and a particular business can reach them, serve them, and retain sufficient value after competitors, suppliers, and substitutes respond. A good founding opportunity adds a further condition: this founder can earn an initial position before running out of cash, time, or credibility.}
