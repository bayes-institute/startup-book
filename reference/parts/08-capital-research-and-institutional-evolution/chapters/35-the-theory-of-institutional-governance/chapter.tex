\ChapterOpener{35}{The Theory of Institutional Governance}{Legal Structure, Corporate Control, and Succession}{How do you structure legal governance, balance commercial sustainability with public-interest mission, and secure regulatory authority?}

\begin{quote}
\itshape
\textbf{Central thesis.} Governance is not an abstract virtue reserved for old institutions. It is the machinery that determines who can commit the company, whose money is at risk, what information must be trusted, who can stop a harmful decision, and how founders preserve legitimate strategic control. Good governance allows an ambitious founder to move quickly without allowing small legal, financial, academic or interpersonal mistakes to become existential risks.
\end{quote}

\section*{Orientation and Scope}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Orientation and Scope}

This chapter establishes a rigorous operating, legal, and institutional theory of corporate governance for knowledge, research, and technical enterprises. Governance is not an ornamental exercise in corporate bureaucracy; it is the constitutional operating system determining corporate authority, capital allocation, regulatory legitimacy, and fiduciary accountability. An ambitious founder who scales without clean ownership registers, explicit reserved matters, dual-control financial gates, and independent academic authority risks fatal dilution, personal statutory liability, or institutional illegitimacy.

\textbf{Important legal boundary.} This chapter is an operating and negotiation framework, not legal, tax, investment, or regulatory advice. Indian Companies Act, FEMA, UGC, state private-university, FCRA, data protection, and sector-specific obligations depend on legal form, activity, thresholds, amendments, and jurisdiction. The exact operative law and filings must be verified with qualified Indian company secretarial, legal, accounting, and academic-regulatory advisers before action. \textbf{[Evidence]} identifies attributed official-source context; \textbf{[Model]} indicates illustrative economics; \textbf{[Decision rule]} indicates founder operating recommendations. All monetary amounts are hypothetical INR unless an official statutory figure is explicitly cited.
