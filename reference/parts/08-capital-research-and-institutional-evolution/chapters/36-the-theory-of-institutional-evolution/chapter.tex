\ChapterOpener{36}{The Theory of Institutional Evolution}{Long-Horizon Strategy, Generational Leadership, and Renewal}{How do you ensure an institution survives its founder, continuously reinvents itself, and thrives for centuries?}

\begin{quote}
\itshape
The objective is not to turn a course company into a large course company. It is to use the advantages of a small, profitable, adaptive education business to assemble the capital, talent, legitimacy, intellectual culture, and public purpose of a serious research institution - without allowing the 1st business to consume the possibility of the 2nd.
\end{quote}

\section*{Orientation, Scope, and Adaptation Note}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Orientation, Scope, and Adaptation Note}

This closing chapter develops and substantially extends the founder's 2026 strategic treatise, \textit{The Theory of Institutional Evolution: From a Laptop and a Profitable Learning Business to a Research Institution That Can Outlive Its Founder}. The source treatise's long-form narrative, staged Bayes Institute framework, analytical calculations, and empirical references are fully retained as the core foundation. Part XVI and Appendices K-P extend the source into an explicit, rigorous institutional decision laboratory. Accordingly, this is an integrated, exhaustive adaptation of the manuscript, codifying how an enterprise moves through changing capabilities, preserves its mission, chooses among alternative futures, renews itself, and eventually becomes independent of its founding generation.

\subsection*{Evidence Convention and Strategic Status}
This chapter is a founder's strategic operating manual and decision theory, not a speculative forecast, legal opinion, valuation, or investment solicitation. All monetary values are presented in nominal Indian rupees (\textnormal{₹}), where 1 crore = \textnormal{₹}10 million (\textnormal{₹}1,00,00,000) and 1 lakh = \textnormal{₹}100,000. Throughout the analysis, 4 explicit evidence standards govern assertions:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1.5pt]
  \item \textbf{[Evidence]}: Documented statutory, regulatory, historical, or external empirical fact.
  \item \textbf{[Model]}: Explicit hypothetical calculation, baseline projection, or analytical stress test.
  \item \textbf{[Hypothesis]}: Actionable strategic proposition requiring empirical validation before commitment.
  \item \textbf{[Decision rule]}: Recommended institutional policy, governance constraint, or gatekeeper protocol.
\end{itemize}
