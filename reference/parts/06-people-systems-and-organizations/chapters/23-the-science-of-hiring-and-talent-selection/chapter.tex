\section*{Orientation and Scope}

The 1st 5 parts of this book explored how founders identify, create, price, sell, and repeatedly deliver value. Part VI begins with a more difficult transition: the work must eventually be performed by people other than the founder. That transition is constrained by the quality of decisions about whom to trust with it.

\textbf{Scope and evidence note.} This is a general theory of selection for founders of educational institutions, service firms, technology companies, industrial businesses, and other organizations. It draws on industrial-organizational psychology, psychometrics, decision theory, and original professional guidance. Research findings are distinguished from proposed founder practices. All Bayes Institute hiring plans, roles, numerical evaluations, candidate cases, and decisions below are \textbf{hypothetical designs}, not evidence of actual Bayes recruitment, employees, or hiring outcomes. Jurisdiction-specific comments are orientation, not legal advice. The chapter builds on ideas in the earlier essay \textit{The Theory of Educational Product Economics and Hiring} but develops selection as its own discipline. Chapter 24 addresses the specialized execution of hiring teachers, AI engineers, and other technical talent in much greater occupational detail.

\textbf{Reading map.} Part I: hiring as a problem of inference, production, and trust; Part II: analyze the work before analyzing the applicant; Part III: candidate sourcing, access, and choice sets; Part IV: the science of assessment, measurement, and validity; Part V: structured assessments, interviews, and work samples; Part VI: fairness, access, privacy, and AI-mediated hiring; Part VII: hiring decisions, expected value, and organizational learning; Part VIII: how selection science changes across different kinds of work; Part IX: the continuing case of Bayes Institute's 1st talent decisions; Part X: a founder's practical operating system for talent selection; followed by practical appendices and research foundations.
