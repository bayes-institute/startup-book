\subsection*{Appendix B: A 45-Minute Credibility Interview}

When diagnosing why qualified prospective buyers hesitate to commit, conduct a structured 45-minute qualitative interview. Adhere strictly to the phased protocol below, recording the buyer's unvarnished language without attempting to argue or sell:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Minutes 0 to 10: Reconstruct the Last Purchase Decision.} Prompt the participant to walk through their most recent purchase of a professional technical training program, enterprise software tool, or specialist advisory service:
\begin{quote}
\textit{``What specific triggering event forced you to look for a solution? What personal or organizational capital was at risk? Which 3 alternative providers did you evaluate?''}
\end{quote}
\item \textbf{Minutes 10 to 20: Map Hidden Quality Attributes.} Identify which elements of the service were impossible to evaluate prior to purchase:
\begin{quote}
\textit{``At the moment of signing, what was your single largest fear? What information source did you trust most (peer recommendations, public documentation, or direct trials)? What would have been an unacceptable post-purchase surprise?''}
\end{quote}
\item \textbf{Minutes 20 to 30: Present the Inspectable Proof Asset.} Present a factual, unadorned artifact from your curriculum (e.g., a sample 4-page assignment rubric or an unedited video code review):
\begin{quote}
\textit{``Read this artifact silently for 5 minutes. Looking only at this document, what does it prove to you about our capability? What crucial questions does it leave completely unanswered?''}
\end{quote}
Do not defend the document or explain away misunderstandings; log the participant's exact interpretation.
\item \textbf{Minutes 30 to 40: Audit Structural Safeguards.} Probe terms, payment structures, and operational commitments:
\begin{quote}
\textit{``If you were considering this 6-week studio, what payment structure would make the risk acceptable? Would a prorated exit policy, a milestone installment plan, or a live diagnostic challenge influence your willingness to commit?''}
\end{quote}
\item \textbf{Minutes 40 to 45: Identify the Decisive Missing Fact.} Conclude with the core diagnostic question:
\begin{quote}
\textit{``If you could verify 1 single factual truth about our operations before making a decision, what fact would instantly tip your choice?''}
\end{quote}
\end{itemize}

Document the exact phrasing used. If 4 out of 5 interviewees identify the exact same missing fact (such as reviewer turnaround reliability), that insight dictates the next operational evidence object to construct.
