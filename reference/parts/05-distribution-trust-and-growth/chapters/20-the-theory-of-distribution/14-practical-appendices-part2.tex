\subsection*{Appendix E: 1st Content Library for a Technical Venture}

An early-stage technical venture builds institutional credibility by publishing a compact, authoritative library of 5 canonical assets rather than hundreds of ephemeral blog posts:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{The Foundational Failure Teardown:} An exhaustive mathematical and architectural post-mortem analyzing why standard retrieval-augmented generation prototypes fail in enterprise production environments, complete with runnable test benchmarks.
  \item \textbf{The Core Laboratory Module:} A complete, interactive instructional notebook featuring runnable code, explicit learning objectives, and edge-case synthetic data generators.
  \item \textbf{The Diagnostic Evaluation Rubric:} A standardized technical scorecard that senior practitioners can use internally to grade their systems across latency, hallucination rates, and semantic drift.
  \item \textbf{The Comparative Architecture Matrix:} A rigorous, unbiased evaluation of commercial versus open-source model judges, detailing computational cost, inference latency, and failure distributions.
  \item \textbf{The Enterprise Implementation Guide:} An evidence-rich architectural manual detailing security boundaries, data governance standards, and automated regression testing.
\end{enumerate}

Every asset in this library is assigned an explicit operational question, a target practitioner seniority level, an annual technical maintenance owner, and an uncoerced next action.

\subsection*{Appendix F: Attribution and Measurement Honesty}

To ensure analytical integrity across executive reporting, Bayes Institute enforces 6 measurement governance standards:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Explicit Dashboard Definitions:} Every reported metric must document its underlying data lineage. An ``attributed conversion'' on an ad network must never be reported as an ``incremental sale'' without holdout evidence.
  \item \textbf{Brand Search Isolation:} Branded search ad conversions must be reported in a separate ledger category from non-branded discovery queries to prevent claiming credit for existing customer demand.
  \item \textbf{Survey Triangulation:} Self-reported discovery surveys must be collected during onboarding and cross-referenced against digital server access logs.
  \item \textbf{Founder Labor Imputation:} Every marketing and sales motion that consumes founder hours must report fully loaded economic CAC alongside cash CAC.
  \item \textbf{Absolute Counts Before Percentages:} In cohorts smaller than 50 learners, performance reports must state absolute integer counts (e.g. ``3 out of 10 applicants'') before quoting percentages to prevent false precision.
  \item \textbf{Sensitivity Stress Testing:} Marketing budget expansions cannot be authorized based on historical average returns without modeling marginal cost increases and reviewer step costs.
\end{enumerate}

  \item \textbf{Supplied Treatise (2026):} \textit{The Theory of Distribution and Trust} (25 October 2026). Foundational frameworks distinguishing promotion, practical distribution, and transactions; multi-ledger marketing controls; and attention-constrained founder economics.
  \item \textbf{Blume Ventures (2024):} \textit{GTM Field Notes: B2C Edition}, edited by Rohit Kaul and Dhruv Trehan. Core practitioner frameworks including Harish Narayanan's ``who/what/how'' sequence; Vamsi Krishna's alignment of marketing spend with operational delivery capacity; Arindam Paul's speed-versus-scale channel matrix; and Nitin Agarwal's funnel diagnostics.
  \item \textbf{Blume Ventures (2025):} \textit{GTM Field Notes: B2B Edition}, edited by Rohit Kaul and Dhruv Trehan. Essential enterprise frameworks including Ravi Kaushik Rajagopal's outbound discipline and persona-problem-impact mapping; Abishek Murthy's channel experimentation; Sairam Krishnan's content-led inbound; Nishchal Dua and Prakhar Jain's event-led motion; and Anand Venkatraman's partner economics.

  \item \textbf{Les Binet and Peter Field (2013):} \textit{The Long and the Short of It: Balancing Short and Long-Term Marketing Strategies}, Institute of Practitioners in Advertising (IPA). Foundational empirical research demonstrating the distinct mechanics, time constants, and economic returns of short-term sales activation versus long-term brand building.
  \item \textbf{Byron Sharp (2010):} \textit{How Brands Grow: What Marketers Don't Know}, Oxford University Press. Empirical demonstration of mental availability, physical availability, and customer acquisition dynamics.
  \item \textbf{Google, Alistair Rennie, and Jonny Protheroe (2020):} \textit{Navigating Purchase Behavior in the Messy Middle}, Think with Google. Extensive behavioral research illustrating the non-linear, trigger-dependent nature of modern consumer and professional evaluation journeys.
  \item \textbf{Andrew Chen (2021):} \textit{The Cold Start Problem: How to Start and Scale Network Effects}, Harper Business. Analysis of atomic market density, multi-sided marketplace liquidity, and the structural limitations of network-driven distribution.
\end{itemize}
