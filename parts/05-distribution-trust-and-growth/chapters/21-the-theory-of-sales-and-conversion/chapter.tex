\begin{quote}

\section*{Orientation and Scope}

Chapters 19 and 20 developed credibility and access. This chapter examines the commercial conversation, the purchasing process, and the transition from prospect to customer. Chapter 22 begins after the contract or enrollment, when delivering value, earning renewal, and creating advocacy take center stage. Together they form the go-to-market architecture.

\textbf{Reading map.} Part I: why buying is difficult; Part II: psychology, diagnosis, and qualification; Part III: consultative sales and value demonstration; Part IV: negotiation, procurement, and contracts; Part V: pipeline and conversion mathematics; Part VI: industry-specific sales architecture; Part VII: Bayes Institute; Part VIII: founder field manual; Part IX: advanced deal and negotiation cases; appendices.

\textbf{Evidence and attribution.} This chapter draws on the supplied \textit{Theory of Distribution and Trust}, the Blume Ventures \textit{GTM Field Notes: B2B Edition} (2025) and \textit{B2C Edition} (2024), and economic contract theory. Reports of specific tactics from Locus, Chargebee, Vymo, Adyen, and other contributors describe their specific settings; they are not guaranteed templates for Bayes. Every numerical sales illustration below is hypothetical unless specifically identified as a source-reported example.
