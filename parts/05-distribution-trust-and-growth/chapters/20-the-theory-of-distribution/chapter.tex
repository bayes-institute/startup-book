\section*{Orientation and Scope}

Chapter 19 explained why people might believe a promise. This chapter examines how such a promise reaches the people for whom it matters. Sales execution belongs principally to Chapter 21; post-purchase compounding belongs to Chapter 22. Distribution includes both the economics of attention and the design of practical access: not merely advertising.

\textbf{Source note.} This chapter uses the supplied \textit{Theory of Distribution and Trust} and the 2 Blume Ventures \textit{GTM Field Notes} collections (B2C Edition 2024; B2B Edition 2025). Blume contributor cases are attributed by name; their suggested ratios, channel choices, and performance observations are context-specific, not statistical laws. All Bayes calculations are hypothetical experiments and analytical models, not evidence of historical traction.
