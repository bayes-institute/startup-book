\section*{Orientation and Scope}

Chapters 19 to 21 explained belief, access, and the commercial decision. This chapter completes go-to-market by studying delivery, adoption, renewal, loyalty, advocacy, referrals, and the systems that allow reputation to compound over time. It is also the bridge from selling value to preserving it across time.

\textbf{Source note.} This chapter synthesizes the supplied \textit{Theory of Distribution and Trust}, both Blume Ventures \textit{GTM Field Notes} collections (B2C Edition 2024; B2B Edition 2025), and established literature on service quality, retention, and customer relationships. Source-reported company experiences from Vymo, Whatfix, and other contributors describe specific settings, not universal templates for Bayes. All numerical Bayes cohort, renewal, and referral examples are hypothetical analytical models, not historical observations.

\textbf{Reading map.} Part I: the post-purchase economic system; Part II: customer success and realized value; Part III: retention measurement and economics; Part IV: loyalty, referral, and word of mouth; Part V: repeat purchases, expansion, and relationship design; Part VI: cross-industry retention architectures; Part VII: the Bayes Institute relationship system; Part VIII: founder operating method; Part IX: advanced retention analytics; appendices and research sources.
