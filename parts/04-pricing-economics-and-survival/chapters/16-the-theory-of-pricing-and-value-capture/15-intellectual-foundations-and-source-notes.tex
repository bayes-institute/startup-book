\section*{Intellectual Foundations and Source Notes}

The analytical literature below provides historical, theoretical, and empirical grounding for value capture, pricing structures, and reference pricing.

\paragraph{Foundational references.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Andreas Hinterhuber (2008), ``Customer Value-Based Pricing Strategies: Why Companies Resist,'' \emph{Journal of Business Strategy}, 29(4), 41 to 50.
  \item Robert J. Dolan and Hermann Simon (1996), \emph{Power Pricing: How Managing Price Transforms the Bottom Line}, Free Press.
  \item Thomas T. Nagle, John E. Hogan, and Joseph Zale, \emph{The Strategy and Tactics of Pricing: A Guide to Growing More Profitably}, Prentice Hall.
  \item James C. Anderson, James A. Narus, and Wouter van Rossum (2006), ``Customer Value Propositions in Business Markets,'' \emph{Harvard Business Review}, 84(3), 90 to 99.
  \item Eric T. Anderson and Duncan I. Simester (2003), ``Effects of \$9 Price Endings on Retail Sales: Evidence from Field Experiments,'' \emph{Quantitative Marketing and Economics}, 1(1), 93 to 110.
  \item Gurumurthy Kalyanaram and Russell S. Winer (1995), ``Empirical Generalizations from Reference Price Research,'' \emph{Marketing Science}, 14(3 supplement), G161 to G169.
  \item Daniel Kahneman, Jack L. Knetsch, and Richard H. Thaler (1986), ``Fairness as a Constraint on Profit Seeking: Entitlements in the Market,'' \emph{American Economic Review}, 76(4), 728 to 741.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Commercial practice and standards.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Stripe, ``SaaS Pricing Models 101,'' descriptive practitioner guide on recurring, seat-based, and usage billing.
  \item IFRS Foundation, IFRS 15 \emph{Revenue from Contracts with Customers}, core principles governing the distinction between price, contract consideration, and recognized revenue.
  \item Founder-supplied companion essay: \emph{The Theory of Pricing and Value Capture} (October 2026), an original operational document whose 14 premium sources, counterfactual framing, price waterfall, and proof-based architecture are systematized throughout this chapter.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Limitations.}
These sources discuss different markets, regulatory environments, and commercial arrangements. The equations in this chapter require specified assumptions; numerical figures and conversion rates are illustrative. Pricing studies show associations or treatment effects under specific conditions, not universal guarantees for Bayes Institute or any other new enterprise.
