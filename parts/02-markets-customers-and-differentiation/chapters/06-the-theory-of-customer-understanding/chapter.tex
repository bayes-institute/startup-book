\ChapterOpener{6}{The Theory of Customer Understanding}{How to Discover What People Are Trying to Accomplish, Why They Make the Choices They Make, and What Evidence Should Change a Founder's Mind}{To understand a customer is not merely to know what they say they want. It is to construct a progressively testable explanation of what they are trying to change, why it matters now, how they cope today, what alternatives they seriously consider, who can authorize the decision, what they must sacrifice, what prevents action, and what observable behavior would demonstrate genuine demand. A good customer theory predicts decisions; a bad one only sounds empathetic.}
