\section*{Appendix F: The Entry-Wedge Design Memo}

Complete these 10 prompts before launching any new commercial entry:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Who is the exact customer?} Define the user, economic buyer, and acute trigger.
    \item \textbf{What complete job will be performed?} Specify the finished outcome and explicit exclusions.
    \item \textbf{Why is the incumbent insufficient for this situation?} Identify root failure causes.
    \item \textbf{Why might this unknown provider be believed?} Name inspectable, verifiable proof.
    \item \textbf{What is the minimum commitment required?} State time, money, and approval gates.
    \item \textbf{What must the company build to deliver?} Specify faculty, rubrics, software, and checks.
    \item \textbf{What could go wrong?} Model refunds, reputation damage, cash drag, and founder strain.
    \item \textbf{What is the observable success criterion?} Define paid enrollment and outcome benchmarks.
    \item \textbf{What would refute the thesis?} State the behavioral condition that triggers a stop.
    \item \textbf{What asset remains after success or failure?} List reusable curriculum, code, and insights.
\end{enumerate}
