\ChapterOpener{7}{The Theory of the Beachhead}{How to Choose the First Customers You Can Genuinely Win, Build a Position Strong Enough to Matter, and Expand Without Losing the Advantage That Made You Possible}{A beachhead is not simply a small target audience. It is the first sufficiently coherent group of buyers for whom you can become a compelling, reachable, economically sustainable choice with the resources and credibility you possess today. A good beachhead is narrow enough to learn and win, substantial enough to matter, and connected enough to what comes next that success creates transferable assets rather than a profitable dead end.}
