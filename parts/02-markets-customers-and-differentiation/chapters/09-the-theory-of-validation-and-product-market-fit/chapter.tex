\ChapterOpener{9}{The Theory of Validation and Product-Market Fit}{How to Discover Whether a Market Genuinely Wants What You Can Deliver, Before Confusing Interest with Demand, Revenue with Success, or Early Traction with a Repeatable Business}{A founder has not validated a business by discovering a plausible problem, collecting compliments, building a product, or even making a few sales. Validation is the progressive reduction of uncertainty about who will commit, for what reason, under which conditions, whether the promised value is delivered, whether those customers return or recommend when appropriate, and whether a new group of comparable customers can be acquired and served at viable economics. Product-market fit is not a badge acquired after a successful launch. It is evidence of a repeatable relationship between a particular market and a particular offer.}
