\section{15 Common Mistakes in Assessing Advantage}

\subsection{Pathologies of Self-Diagnosis}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.},leftmargin=11pt,itemsep=2pt,topsep=1.5pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{Treating rarity as customer value.} A rare combination of skills matters only if it improves a commercially consequential task. Being unusual is not an economic moat.
  \item \textbf{Treating a résumé as a demonstration.} Prestigious logos and degrees attract initial attention but do not establish independent delivery capability.
  \item \textbf{Confusing access with permission.} Possession of phone numbers, student rosters, or corporate email lists does not confer legal or ethical rights to advertise.
  \item \textbf{Confusing the user with the economic buyer.} Loving a workflow solution yourself reveals user pain, but another executive may control the corporate budget.
  \item \textbf{Treating founder enthusiasm as evidence of demand.} A founder's obsession with a mission is completely uncoupled from a customer's urgency to pay.
  \item \textbf{Ignoring the calendar.} A business model that fits your technical skills will fail if it requires daytime customer response windows you cannot provide.
  \item \textbf{Treating borrowed resources as owned resources.} \textbf{Corporate teams, brand prestige, licenses, and relationships do not accompany you into an independent company.}
  \item \textbf{Using heuristic scores as calibrated science.} A scorecard structures qualitative reflection; it is not a statistical prediction of revenue.
  \item \textbf{Selecting the largest market instead of the accessible experiment.} A ₹50,000 crore market is a lethal starting point if you lack access, credibility, or capital to reach 10 customers.
  \item \textbf{Mistaking opening advantage for permanent defensibility.} \textbf{A brilliant founding teacher is not a scalable university; a connected founder is not an automated distribution system.}
  \item \textbf{Discounting unglamorous domain expertise.} Mastery of payroll tax, regulatory compliance, plant maintenance, or freight reconciliation creates immense value without online glamour.
  \item \textbf{Assuming you must quit employment or recruit a cofounder on Day Zero.} These are structural choices, not mandatory initiation rituals. Decide them based on verified bottlenecks.
  \item \textbf{Falling in love with the 1st plausible market.} Real capability in 1 sector does not imply the 1st product concept you imagine there deserves years of capital.
  \item \textbf{Using past success as universal proof.} A triumph achieved under a specific manager or brand may not transfer. Isolate the specific mechanism that drove performance.
  \item \textbf{Failing to update the self-assessment.} The ledger is an updating instrument. A rejected pilot reveals positioning errors; a referral reveals an unsuspected strength.
\end{enumerate}
