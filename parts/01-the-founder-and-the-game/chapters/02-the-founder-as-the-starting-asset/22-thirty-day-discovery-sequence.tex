\section{A 30-Day Discovery Sequence}

\subsection{Calendar Roadmap for Employed Founders}

The protocol below is concrete and designed for a founder with limited cash and an existing full-time job.

\subsection{Phase-by-Phase Execution}

\textbf{Days 1 to 3: Reconstruct Empirical Receipts} \\
Collect 10 concrete episodes of past performance. Write task-level statements for your 5 most credible skills. List 3 areas of domain workflow knowledge. Isolate personal contribution from corporate employer resources. \\
\textit{Output:} Personal advantage ledger with evidence levels and uncertainties.

\textbf{Days 4 to 6: External Triangulation} \\
Interview 6 to 10 informed people without telegraphing your preferred conclusions. Elicit specific episodes of past failure and demonstrated strengths. \\
\textit{Output:} Corroborated strengths-and-gaps audit.

\textbf{Days 7 to 9: Define Financial and Legal Gates} \\
Complete your household cash-flow statement and 6-month reserve calculation. Map normal versus bad weeks. Audit IP agreements and moonlighting policies. \\
\textit{Output:} Affordable-loss budget, weekly calendar, and constraint register.

\textbf{Days 10 to 12: Construct the Idea Map} \\
Generate 10 to 15 intersections of your assets, reachable customers, and severe pains. Filter them into 3 to 5 opportunity cards. \\
\textit{Output:} Ranked investigation list with explicit inclusion and exclusion rationales.

\textbf{Days 13 to 18: Discovery Conversations} \\
Conduct 5 to 10 structured interviews with target buyers. Ask:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=11pt,itemsep=1pt,topsep=1pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item When did this problem last occur, and what did it cost?
  \item What tools or manual workarounds do you currently deploy?
  \item Who signs the purchase order to fix this?
  \item What would make switching solutions unacceptable?
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Avoid the polite trap of asking ``Would you buy my product?'' Inquire exclusively into past behavior, current budgets, and existing painful workarounds.} \\
\textit{Output:} Customer problem evidence log.

\textbf{Days 19 to 23: Build 1 Small Valuable Artifact} \\
Create the smallest honest prototype or diagnostic: an automated workflow, a diagnostic memo, or a sample lesson and unseen assessment. Keep it representative of core value. \\
\textit{Output:} Tested work sample, verified delivery hours, and external peer review.

\textbf{Days 24 to 27: Make a Bounded Real Offer} \\
Offer a bounded paid pilot with clear deliverables or request a formal stakeholder evaluation agreement. \textbf{Never pretend that an unpriced free demo proves demand; willingness to pay is demonstrated only when a customer commits scarce budget or formal time.} \\
\textit{Output:} Transactional evidence (signed deposit or documented rejection reasons).

\textbf{Days 28 to 30: The Founder-Position Memo} \\
Draft a concise 2-page memo outlining:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.},leftmargin=11pt,itemsep=1pt,topsep=1pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item Which 3 capabilities are empirically demonstrated.
  \item Which buyer personas and workflows you genuinely understand.
  \item The cash and hours you can responsibly commit.
  \item Which opportunity has the highest evidentiary support.
  \item The 3 most probable failure mechanisms and the next falsification test.
\end{enumerate}
