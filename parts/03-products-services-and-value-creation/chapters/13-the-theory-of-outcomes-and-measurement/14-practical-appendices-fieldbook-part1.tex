\subsection*{Appendix C: Assessment Blueprint and Rater Calibration}

Create a matrix crossing \textbf{capability dimensions} with \textbf{task evidence}. Every essential dimension should be represented by a defensible task or observation; use more than 1 where the stakes justify it. Write the independence and assistance rules first. Create sample weak, competent, and strong artifacts with explanations of why the rubric classifies them that way.

Before high-stakes use, ask 2 qualified reviewers to score the same representative set independently. Compare not only totals but dimension-level disagreements. Reconcile ambiguous standards, document alternative valid approaches, and repeat until the process produces acceptable consistency for the intended decision. Keep borderline cases available for later review and monitor whether changes in assessment difficulty alter reported outcomes.
