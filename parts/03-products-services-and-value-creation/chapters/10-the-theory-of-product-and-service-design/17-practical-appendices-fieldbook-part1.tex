\section*{Appendix C: The Minimum Complete Experience Test}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix C: The Minimum Complete Experience Test}

Before deploying an early offering to paying customers, the founding team must verify that the product represents a viable value engine rather than an irresponsible prototype. Audit the design against these 10 criteria:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Autonomous completion:} Can an authentic customer complete a consequential job without relying on continuous founder coaching?
\item \textbf{Explicit prerequisite disclosure:} Are necessary prior skills, hardware configurations, time commitments, and data access rules stated in advance?
\item \textbf{End-to-end journey coherence:} Are all stages from initial transaction to final verification mapped and operationally supported?
\item \textbf{Preservation of the causal core:} Does the minimal prototype retain the precise mechanism responsible for value creation (e.g., rigorous feedback rubrics)?
\item \textbf{Inspectable outcome criteria:} Can the user independently verify whether their final output meets the promised performance standard?
\item \textbf{Containment of predictable failures:} Are detection and recovery protocols established for foreseeable technical outages or missed deadlines?
\item \textbf{Proportionate safety and privacy:} Are data handling, privacy consents, and system security controls appropriate to the stakes of the domain?
\item \textbf{Full accounting of hidden labor:} Are all manual tasks, reviewer hours, and administrative chores tracked and valued at market rates?
\item \textbf{Truthful scope alignment:} Does published promotional material promise only what the current operational system can reliably deliver?
\item \textbf{Falsifiable learning yield:} Will the deployment generate concrete evidence capable of disproving the founder's core hypotheses?
\end{enumerate}

A negative response to any criterion does not require abandoning the venture. It mandates narrowing the scope, adding missing safeguards, or stating operational boundaries openly to the customer.
